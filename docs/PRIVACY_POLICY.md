# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

TripMap is a travel management app that helps users organize trips, reservations, locations, itinerary items, and related notes.

## Information We Collect

TripMap may store the information that users enter into the app, including:

- Trip titles, destinations, start dates, and end dates
- Hotel, attraction, tour, and transportation details
- Addresses, dates, times, prices, booking sites, confirmation numbers, URLs, and notes
- Images selected by the user, such as ticket images or QR code images

When Firebase cloud sync is configured and enabled, TripMap uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication and may store trip data in Firebase Firestore and selected attachment images in Firebase Storage.

When Firebase is not configured or cloud sync is not used, TripMap stores trip data locally on the user's device.

## How We Use Information

We use the information only to provide the app's travel planning and itinerary management features, including:

- Displaying trips and spots in the app
- Showing planned places on a map
- Organizing itinerary items by date and time
- Opening official or booking URLs selected by the user
- Syncing data through Firebase when configured

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

We do not share user data with advertisers.

When cloud sync is configured, data may be processed by Google Firebase as a backend service provider. Map display may use Google Maps services.

## Photos and Attachments

TripMap asks for photo library access only when the user chooses to attach an image to a trip spot. The selected image is used for the user's own travel record, such as a ticket image or QR code image.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored data remains on the user's device until the user edits or deletes it in the app, removes the app, or clears app data.

Cloud-synced data remains in Firebase until it is deleted from the app or deletion is requested.

To request deletion of cloud-synced data, contact the developer at kazu20040127@gmail.com.

## Security

TripMap uses platform and Firebase security features where available. Data transmitted to Firebase is encrypted in transit.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so users should avoid storing unnecessary highly sensitive information in notes or attachments.

## Children's Privacy

TripMap is not designed for children. It is intended for general travel planning use.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted with a new "Last updated" date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or deletion requests, contact the developer at kazu20040127@gmail.com.
